**Warp 9** is an online training platform created by TJ Addams that offers fast-paced technology courses, including the popular "[Ultimate Docker Fast-Track Beginner to Advanced](https://warp-9.com)" course. 

### About Warp 9 Docker Training
* **Goal:** Teach Docker quickly to beginners and advanced users, often in under 90 minutes.
* **Topics Covered:** Container basics, Docker images, Dockerfiles, setting up engines on Linux (like CentOS), and running containers anywhere.
* **Availability:** You can find these programs directly on the [Warp 9 Training Platform](https://warp-9.com) or via platforms like [Udemy](https://udemy.com).
